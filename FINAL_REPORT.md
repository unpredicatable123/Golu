# Final report: Navaratri Golu Darshan

This report follows the structure SPEC §7 asks for. Design decisions are numbered D1–D64 in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md), and [README.md](README.md) explains how to run the project.

## What was built

A scroll-driven devotional web experience of a nine-step Tamil Navaratri Golu. Every visual is generated by code.

**Blender pipeline** (Python, headless Blender 4.5 LTS):
- **Room:** a procedural pooja room with a niche wall, the nine-step stand with silk drapes and brass trims, marigold and jasmine garlands, mango-leaf thoranam, banana stems, kuthuvilakkus and a mirror-curve sikku kolam.
- **Dolls:** 73 clay golu dolls in the painted-clay style, built by a parametric doll factory. Each follows the iconography rules in [ICONOGRAPHY.md](ICONOGRAPHY.md): skin, arm count, attributes, vahanas and forehead marks, all checked by `check_iconography.py`.
- **Story sets:**
  - PSLV and launch gantry, Chandrayaan-3
  - wedding mandapam
  - thinai field
  - Chettiar shop
  - bullock cart
  - Kaliya pool, Govardhana hill
  - Ashta Lakshmi lotus seats, Gaja Lakshmi elephants
- **Renders:** a nine-step camera fly-through with a finale pull-back, an entrance door sequence, and 12 transparent ritual and prasadam sprites.

**Web app** (SvelteKit 2, Svelte 5, TypeScript, Tailwind 4, GSAP, Howler):
- **Entrance:** carved temple doors, a large bell on Enter, and the doors swinging open into a golden bloom.
- **Fly-through:** scroll-scrubbed 1080p frames, step title cards, a lamp step navigator, and hotspots that open bilingual story cards.
- **Aarthi and camphor:** the plate circles exactly 3 times clockwise and 3 times anticlockwise with a live canvas flame on the wick. Bells ring in rhythm, scroll is locked during the ritual, and the camphor flame goes out at the end.
- **Prasadam:** a banana-leaf tray with the configured trio, steam, a petal shower and the bilingual blessing.
- **Audio:** the owner-supplied Om chant streams in after Enter and dips during rituals. Bells are synthesised in the browser.
- **Accessibility:** a reduced-motion key-frame view, full keyboard operation, and screen-reader announcements.

## Final render settings and time

The planned final render was Cycles at 96 samples. On this laptop (4-core CPU, integrated Intel GPU) Cycles would have taken days, so the owner approved the **high** tier (D58, D65).

| Item | Setting |
|---|---|
| Engine | EEVEE (Blender 4.5.14 LTS), 16 TAA samples, full-resolution shadows, AgX "Medium High Contrast" |
| Resolution | 1920 × 1080 |
| Fly-through rendering | Every frame of the step transitions and the finale; every second frame of the slow holds |
| Missing hold frames | Each real hold frame is shown for two frames (D65). Motion interpolation was tried and dropped: it ghosted overlapping dolls. |
| Encoding | WebP: desktop 1920 px at q85, mobile 960 px at q78, light unsharp mask; door video VP9 and H.264 |
| Sprites | Cycles, 32 samples + OpenImageDenoise, 1024 px (petals 256 px), transparent, with a faded contact shadow |

## Known limitations

1. **EEVEE instead of Cycles** for the final frames, because of the hardware. `render_all.sh --quality final` still works on a GPU machine.
2. **Held frames in the slow holds.** 432 of the 1,128 frames repeat the previous real render, because the deadline left no time to render them (about 5½ h on this laptop). Scrubbing through a hold therefore updates at an effective 12 fps. Every image shown is crisp, and the fast moves (transitions, finale, doors) render every frame. To upgrade: `render_steps.py --quality high --frames 2,4,…,96`, then `encode_frames.sh --quality high --force`.
3. **Kalki and Durga have no painted eyes** in the shipped frames: a rider-height bug (D66), found after rendering. Their info dots are fixed. The next render of steps 2 and 9 picks up the face fix.
4. **Mobile Lighthouse is 64–67, not the planned 85.** Lighthouse's simulated slow-4G phone waits for scripts and the Tamil font before the title's final paint. Desktop scores 92. Real phones on normal networks load the entrance in about 1 MB.
5. **60 fps on a mid-range Android phone was not measured on a real device.** Smoothness was measured in desktop Chrome on this laptop (see Verification).
6. **Frames are not in git** (D46): `web/static/frames*`, the doors, the props and `frames.json` are git-ignored generated output. Deploy from this machine's `web/build`, or regenerate with the render and encode scripts.
7. **The prasadam composite matches the configured third dish (puliyodarai).** Changing `prasadam.third_dish` needs `render_props.py --only tray_composite`.
8. **The Om recording's licence** has to be confirmed by the owner before public deployment (ASSETS_NEEDED.md).
